# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want collaboration here to be technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss people and their work respectfully;
- critique claims with reproducible evidence rather than personal attacks;
- make room for questions and good-faith correction;
- disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and relevant affiliations;
- protect private infrastructure and personal information; and
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing,
  or sustained disruption.

These expectations apply in project repositories, reviews, issue discussions,
community events, and private communications undertaken on behalf of the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict a
participant. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@solidsf.com`. Do not include
credentials, private host details, or unnecessary personal information. A
maintainer named in a report will not handle that report.
